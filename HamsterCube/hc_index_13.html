Hamster Cube

The hamster is trapped in a cube of glass tunnels. If it rolls into a blue hole it gets stuck halfway: zoom out and swipe to tip the cube so the floor of the next tunnel points down. Red holes cost a life. Press JUMP to jump: the faster you roll, the longer the jump (green = long enough for a hole). Watch out for holes in the floor, cracked glass and grey hamsters that chase you. Run in a hamster wheel to charge the battery that opens a door or runs a lift (⚡ = fully charged). Collect sunflower seeds (10 fit in the mouth). Stand still and the button says SPIT: three hits make a grey hamster dizzy. Push wooden boxes, press wall buttons to open gates, and use keys to let steel balls out. Find the exit!
