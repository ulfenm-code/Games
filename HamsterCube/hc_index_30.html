The story

The hamster is trapped in a plastic cube. Inside the cube it is forced to take part in experiment after experiment, to be trained into an obedient special forces hamster. Many grey hamsters have already given in and do exactly as they are told.

But our hamster, together with you, has another goal. And so a dangerous mission begins. Our hamster rolls its way through the cube.

Your mission: help the hamster get out of the cube by tilting and turning it.

How to play

Roll

Tilt your phone to roll: forward to go, sideways to turn. No tilt sensor? Drag on the screen.

Jump

Press JUMP while rolling. The faster you roll, the longer the jump: green means long enough for a hole.

Blue holes

Roll into a blue hole and you get stuck halfway. Zoom out with 🧊 and swipe to tip the cube so the floor of the next corridor points down.

Red holes

A red hole costs a life. Roll around the small ones and jump over the wide ones.

Seeds and SPIT

Roll over sunflower seeds to collect them (10 fit in the mouth). Stand still and the button says SPIT.

Grey hamsters

They chase you and catch you for a life. Three seeds make one dizzy, or jump over it.

Wheel and battery

Run in a hamster wheel to charge the battery ⚡. A full battery opens a door or runs a lift.

Wooden boxes

Push a box one cell at a time. It breaks a cracked floor and falls into gaps, where you can stand on it.

Buttons and gates

Roll into a wall button with a full battery and its gate opens for good.

Keys and ball doors

Pick up keys 🔑. A key unlocks a ball door, and a steel ball rolls out. It pushes boxes and presses buttons.

Seed machine

Roll into the yellow button of a seed machine and 3 seeds pop into your mouth. While it refills, the button is grey and the yellow ring around it fills up in 30 seconds.

Cracked floor

A cracked floor breaks just after you roll over it. Don't stand still on it!

Walls that break

Three seeds break a wall from the inside, never from the outside.

Floors without walls

Some corridors are just a floor. Roll off the edge and you fall. Under a floor is a red square: stay away from it.

The exit

Roll up to the yellow hatch. Then tap it to open it and roll out of the cube!
